    How to play

    • Players: 2 to 4 per room. Each player gets 13 cards; with fewer than 4 players the unused cards are set aside.
    • Turn order: counter-clockwise (play passes to your right). Calling starts with the player to the right of the dealer, who also leads the first trick. The dealer rotates each round.
    • Round 1: no calls. Everyone plays freely and scores 1 point per trick won.
    • From round 2: each player calls how many tricks they will win: 2 to 12, or Nil (0) to promise winning no tricks.
    • Play: follow the suit that was led if you can. If you can't, play any card. Ranks run A K Q J 10 9 8 7 6 5 4 3 2. Spades are trump: any spade beats any other suit. The trick winner leads the next trick.
    • Scoring: win at least your call → +call (extra tricks add nothing). Win fewer → −call. Nil: win 0 tricks → +10, win any → −10.
    • Winning: the first player to reach 29 or more total points wins. If two players tie for the lead at 29+, play continues.
    • Timer: 20 seconds per turn. If time runs out, one random card from your hand is shown face up to everyone, and the lowest legal card is played for you (lowest rank, spades counted highest). If it was your turn to call, you call 2.
    • Leaving: if a player exits, or drops out and isn't back within 60 seconds, the game ends for everyone. The highest total at that moment wins (ties are shared); the player who left can't win.
